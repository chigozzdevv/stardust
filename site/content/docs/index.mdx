---
title: Overview
description: Automated browser tests for Stellar apps, with a wallet your test controls.
---

Stardust tests Stellar apps in a real browser. Your test drives the app in Chromium the way a user would, and Stardust plays the wallet: it answers the app like the real wallet, signs with throwaway testnet keys, and checks what landed on chain. It runs on your machine or in CI, with no servers, sign-ups or dashboards.

## Wallets

Each user's tab sees one of three wallets.

| `wallet`             | What the page sees                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `'varial'` (default) | [Varial (formerly Freighter)](/docs/wallets/varial), through its own message protocol. Works with `@stellar/freighter-api`, `@stellar/varial-sdk` and Stellar Wallets Kit |
| `'sep60'`            | A wallet announced through [SEP-60](/docs/wallets/sep60) discovery, with the SEP-43 wallet API                                                                            |
| `'none'`             | No wallet at all                                                                                                                                                          |

## What you do

You write `stardust.config.ts` and your test files, then run `stardust test`. Stardust does the rest: it installs Chromium, starts your app, puts the wallet in the page, funds test accounts, answers prompts and checks what landed.

## Safety

- **Never mainnet.** Stardust refuses a network that reports the mainnet passphrase, and nothing is ever signed for mainnet. There's no setting to turn this off.
- **Throwaway keys only.** Every test gets new testnet accounts. Stardust never asks for a real key.
- **Keys stay out of the page.** Signing happens in Node, so your app never sees a secret key.

## What Stardust is not

- A smart-contract testing tool. Test contracts with the Soroban SDK.
- A wallet.
- Production monitoring. Stardust never runs on mainnet.
