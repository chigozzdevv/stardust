When a test fails, the terminal shows the failed check and the wallet's timeline: everything the app asked the wallet and the network, in order. `stardust-results/` keeps a screenshot, a trace and the timeline, so you can see what happened without running the test again.

```text
✘ swap.spec.ts › swaps 10 XLM
  expect(prompt).toLand() failed: transaction FAILED
    result code: txFAILED
    contract error: SlippageExceeded (CA3D…QK7M, #4)
    Transaction 9162…c21e in ledger 5757773

  Timeline:
    0.0s  trader  isAllowed
    0.0s  trader  connect           approved (localhost)
    1.0s  trader  simulateTransaction  220ms
    2.1s  trader  signTransaction   CA3D…QK7M.swap_exact_in(amount_in: 100000000)  approved
    2.4s  trader  sendTransaction   PENDING  916282c2…
    8.1s  trader  landed            FAILED   https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/916282c2…
                                    ledger 5757773, txFAILED
                                    contract error: SlippageExceeded (CA3D…QK7M, #4)
  Screenshot, trace and timeline: stardust-results/swap-swaps-10-XLM/
```

## Read the timeline

Each line shows the seconds since the test started, the user, and what happened:

- **Prompts**, decoded, with the answer. A blocked prompt shows why, such as `rejected (networkMismatch)`.
- **Wallet reads** that need no popup, such as `isAllowed` and `getAddress`.
- **RPC calls** from the page to the RPC URL in your config, such as `simulateTransaction` and `sendTransaction`.
- **Landed** transactions, with the ledger, the contract error, and a stellar.expert link on testnet.
- **Violations** recorded by [strict mode](/docs/check/strict-mode).

Contract arguments are named from the contract's spec. Integers show as raw numbers, so XLM amounts are in stroops.

`Timeline: nothing reached the wallet.` means the app never asked the wallet anything. Check that the app found the wallet, and that the test clicked what starts the flow.

## Open the screenshot and trace

Each failed test gets a folder, such as `stardust-results/swap-swaps-10-XLM/`, with:

- a screenshot of each user's tab when the test ended,
- `trace.zip`, a recording of every step, with the wallet actions next to the clicks,
- `stardust-timeline.json`, the timeline.

Drop `trace.zip` onto [trace.playwright.dev](https://trace.playwright.dev) to open it. It stays on your machine.

Add `stardust-results/` to `.gitignore`. On CI, upload it as an artifact; see [CI](/docs/run/ci).

## Failed or unknown

A check that waits, such as `toLand()`, can fail in two ways:

| You see              | It means                                                        |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `transaction FAILED` | The transaction is in a ledger and failed. The message says why |
| `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`    | The transaction didn't appear on the network within 30 seconds  |

Stardust never reports a transaction it didn't see as failed. `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` usually means the app got the signed transaction but never sent it. For `signAuthEntry`, it means the app or its relayer didn't send the transaction. If the network is slow, pass a longer timeout, such as `toLand({ timeoutMs: 60_000 })`.

If the prompt was rejected, failed or hung, the check fails at once, because nothing was signed:

```text
expect(prompt).toLand() failed: p2 signTransaction is rejected, so nothing was signed and there's nothing to wait for.
```
